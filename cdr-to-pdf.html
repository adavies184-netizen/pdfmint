CDR TO PDF GUIDE

A practical guide to CDR to PDF conversion

CDR is CorelDRAW artwork, while PDF is a fixed-layout document format designed for consistent viewing. Converting between them can make the content easier to open, share or continue working with in the software used by your recipient.

Practical tip: Keep the original CDR file until you have opened and checked the complete PDF result.
01

Why convert CDR to PDF?

A PDF result is useful for sharing a document with a stable page layout. Conversion creates a separate file, so keep the original CDR when you may need its native features later.

02

Prepare your CDR file

Convert text to curves or package fonts when exact typography matters. Remove anything that should not appear in the finished file and use the clearest source available.

03

Check the PDF result

Review every page, link, image and piece of important text. Pay particular attention to text, dimensions, colours, page order and any elements that are important to the document.
