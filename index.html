Got questions?

Find quick answers about PDFMint, document security and using our online tools.

What is PDFMint?

PDFMint is an online document service for editing, signing, organising and converting PDFs directly in your browser. The interface is designed to be quick to learn and easy to use.

Is PDFMint safe to use?

PDFMint is being designed around encrypted transfers, limited file retention and clear privacy controls. Final policies will be published before the service accepts live customer documents.

What tools will PDFMint offer?

The platform will include PDF editing, signatures, compression, merging, splitting, watermarks, page management and popular file conversions.

Do I need to install software?

No. PDFMint runs in modern desktop and mobile browsers. You only need a browser and an internet connection.

Will my original formatting be preserved?

Our goal is to retain text positioning, images, fonts and page dimensions as accurately as possible during editing and conversion.
