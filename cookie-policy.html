Cookie Policy

How PDFMint uses cookies and similar technologies, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 25 July 2026Applies to PDFMint websites, applications and services

On this page

1. What cookies are 2. How we use them 3. Categories 4. Third parties 5. Your choices 6. Browser controls 7. Changes 8. Contact

This policy is written for PDFMint’s launch-stage service and is intended to remain suitable as additional tools, user accounts and paid plans are introduced.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, software development kits and device identifiers.

2. How PDFMint uses cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, maintain sessions, protect accounts, understand performance and measure marketing. Non-essential technologies will be used only where permitted and, where required, after consent.

3. Cookie categories

Strictly necessary

Required for core functions such as security, consent choices, account login, checkout and fraud prevention.

Functional

Remember settings such as language, interface choices or recently used tools.

Analytics

Help us understand aggregated usage, page performance, errors and how visitors move through PDFMint.

Advertising and attribution

May help measure campaigns, limit repeated advertising and understand whether an advertisement resulted in a visit or purchase.

4. Third-party technologies

Some cookies may be set by providers supporting hosting, security, analytics, payments, customer support or advertising. Their use of information may also be governed by their own notices.

5. Your choices

Where a cookie banner or preference centre is available, you can accept, reject or customise non-essential categories. Your choice may be stored so that we do not ask on every visit.

6. Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies. Blocking every cookie may prevent login, checkout, saved preferences or other core features from working correctly.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when technologies, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will appear on this page.
