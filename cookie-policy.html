Cookie Policy

How PDFBreeze uses cookies and similar technologies, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 28 September 2026Applies to PDFBreeze websites, applications and services

On this page

1. What cookies are 2. How we use them 3. Categories 4. Third parties 5. Your choices 6. Browser controls 7. Changes 8. Contact

This policy is written for PDFBreeze’s launch-stage service and is intended to remain suitable as additional tools, user accounts and paid plans are introduced.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, software development kits and device identifiers.

2. How PDFBreeze uses cookies

We use necessary storage and limited first-party operational reporting to operate the site, maintain security, understand genuine use of PDFBreeze tools and support checkout. Your Statistics and Marketing choices control whether Google may use cookies and identifiers for measurement and advertising.

When you reject optional categories, Google storage and advertising permissions remain denied. Google may receive restricted cookieless consent and conversion signals for aggregate modelling. You can continue to use the site after rejecting optional categories.

3. Cookie categories

Strictly necessary

Required for core functions such as security, consent choices, account login, checkout and fraud prevention.

Functional

Remember settings such as language, interface choices or recently used tools.

Analytics

Help us understand aggregated usage, page performance, errors and how visitors move through PDFBreeze.

Advertising and attribution

May help measure campaigns, limit repeated advertising and understand whether an advertisement resulted in a visit or purchase.

4. Third-party technologies

Google tags use Advanced Consent Mode. Before optional consent, or after rejection, consent remains denied and Google receives restricted cookieless signals rather than permission to store advertising or analytics cookies. Providers supporting essential hosting, security, authentication and payments may use strictly necessary storage to deliver those services. Their use of information may also be governed by their own notices.

5. Your choices

You can accept all, reject all or customise non-essential categories from the consent banner. You can review or withdraw your choice at any time using the Cookie settings button shown on the site. Your choice is stored locally so that we do not ask on every visit.

6. Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies. Blocking every cookie may prevent login, checkout, saved preferences or other core features from working correctly.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when technologies, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will appear on this page.
