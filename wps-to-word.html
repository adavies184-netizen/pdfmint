WPS TO WORD GUIDE

A practical guide to WPS to WORD conversion

WPS is a WPS Writer document, while WORD is a Microsoft Word document. Converting between them can make the content easier to open, share or continue working with in the software used by your recipient.

Practical tip: Keep the original WPS file until you have opened and checked the complete WORD result.
01

Why convert WPS to WORD?

A WORD result is useful for editing and reviewing document content. Conversion creates a separate file, so keep the original WPS when you may need its native features later.

02

Prepare your WPS file

Check fonts, page layout, comments and embedded objects. Remove anything that should not appear in the finished file and use the clearest source available.

03

Check the WORD result

Review pagination, tables, headers and font substitution. Pay particular attention to text, dimensions, colours, page order and any elements that are important to the document.
