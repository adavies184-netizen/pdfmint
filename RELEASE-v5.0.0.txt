PDFBreeze Site v5.0.0 - Archives and CAD converters

- Adds direct conversion flows for DWG to DXF, DXF to DWG, RAR to ZIP,
  ZIP to RAR and 7Z to ZIP.
- Each page restricts the device picker to its correct source format.
- Archive and drawing conversions use distinct themed progress stages.
- Successful conversion opens the standard email gate, then downloads the
  prepared file without entering the PDF editor.
- Desktop and mobile navigation remain available on every converter page.

Deploy PDFBreeze Engine v1.8.0 before deploying this site package.
