About us

Simple, secure PDF tools for everyday documents

PDFBreeze is an online application for editing, signing, organising, compressing, converting and managing PDF and document files. We built it so everyday paperwork takes minutes, in any browser, with nothing to install.

Our values

What we care about in every tool we build

Speed

Everyday document jobs should take minutes, not an afternoon. Upload, make your change and download, without installing software.

Accuracy

We aim to keep text positioning, images, fonts and page sizes as close to the original as possible when you edit or convert.

Privacy

Secure connections, clear retention rules and responsible handling of your files. See our privacy policy for the details.
