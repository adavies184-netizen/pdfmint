Dwg to pdf guide

A practical guide to DWG to PDF conversion

DWG is a CAD drawing format commonly used for design data, while PDF is a fixed-layout document format designed for consistent viewing. Converting between them can make the content easier to open, share or continue working with in the software used by your recipient.

01

Why convert DWG to PDF?

A PDF result is useful for sharing a document with a stable page layout. Conversion creates a separate file, so keep the original DWG when you may need its native features later.

02

Prepare your DWG file

Confirm units, layers, external references and drawing scale. Remove anything that should not appear in the finished file and use the clearest source available.

03

Check the PDF result

Review every page, link, image and piece of important text. Pay particular attention to text, dimensions, colours, page order and any elements that are important to the document.

Practical tip: Keep the original DWG file until you have opened and checked the complete PDF result.
